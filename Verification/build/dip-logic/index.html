The 48 flip-flops

These are 175 quad flip-flops, not 157 multiplexers. CD74ACT175E preserves their positive-edge clock, active-low asynchronous clear, complementary outputs and pin numbering. TI lists the DIP-16 part as active. The package conversion retains 48 devices.

The reviewed ACT limits are 2.9–11.5 ns clock-to-Q, 3.3–13 ns clear-to-Q, 2 ns setup/hold and 1 ns reset recovery, at 4.5–5.5 V and −55 to 125 °C with a 50 pF load. ACT input thresholds are 2.0/0.8 V. Existing AC175 timing and analogue results do not establish ACT receiver behaviour.

All 48 positions have reset connected; C1:U1, SQ1:U3 and SQ1:U5 also use complementary outputs. Your HCT574 has no asynchronous clear or complementary outputs, so it cannot directly replace these devices. The netlist has 14 pairs with the same clock/reset and no used complementary outputs, worth examining for eight-bit resettable 273-style registers. That requires rewiring and timing/load checks.
