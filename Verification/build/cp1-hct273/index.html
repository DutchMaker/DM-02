CP1-R2: documented HCT273 timing

Complete digital rerun passed. Physical qualification remains open.

Three CD74HCT273E DIP-20 registers replace SN74HCT273N at AL1 U2 and ME1 U11/U15. All 60 pins, footprints and connectivity match. The design still uses 192 DIP logic/memory ICs, 96 fewer than the 288-package baseline.

TI SCHS174D specifies clock-to-Q and clear-to-Q maxima of 38/40 ns at 4.5 V, −40 to 85 °C and 50 pF. The model retains a conservative 42 ns delay envelope and now checks the required 3 ns data hold.

A fresh ROM boot passed 2,840,175 microcycles and 43,829 IO events, with exact text and zero errors across 48,000 pixels. All three substituted registers have measured post-capture data activity in that digital run.

150 clock profiles, 24 continuous CPU profiles, three all-opcode profiles, exhaustive counter/MAR/register checks and standalone ALU/memory suites passed. The smallest observed hold interval was 243.0 ns. Sampled delay models do not qualify real supply/load behaviour.

Milestones remain 3/5, 3/5 and 8/9. This revision closes the selected HCT273 specification ambiguity; physical acceptance gates remain open.

Remaining work

  • No guaranteed HCT245 IO capacitance; 10 pF per port is a stated allocation.
  • HCT245 data/enable timing is taken at 150 pF; disable timing is only guaranteed at 50 pF.
  • CD74HCT273E clock/clear delays have explicit SCHS174D bounds at 4.5 V, -40..85 C and 50 pF. Actual output loads and input edges remain unqualified.
  • SRAM read table uses 30 pF; output release uses 5 pF. Actual DQ load and turnaround require qualification.
  • HC377 DC compatibility checked at 4.5 V only; full-supply loaded edges remain open.
  • MCP100-450D does not establish valid 4.75 V startup; the staged system retains an external qualified reset input.
  • Oscillator startup, duty at receiver thresholds, power integrity, routing and external IO electronics remain open.
  • Owned 191/245/574 manufacturer/suffix/counts and EPROM programmer/readback remain unresolved.
