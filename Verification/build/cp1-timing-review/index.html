HCT273 timing review

CD74HCT273E evaluated; active CP1 remains unchanged.

TI SCHS174D explicitly specifies clock-to-Q at 38 ns maximum and clear-to-Q at 40 ns maximum, at 4.5 V, −40 to 85 °C and 50 pF. The PDIP-20 ordering entry is Active / Production. These maxima fit inside CP1's 42 ns delay allocation.

The alternative requires 3 ns data hold. CP1's SN74HCT273 model assumes 0 ns, so matching the maximum propagation delay alone cannot qualify the substitution. The study adds a 3 ns monitor while retaining CP1's conservative setup, pulse, recovery and propagation parameters. Unit stimuli check a 2 ns violation and legal 3/4 ns boundaries.

All eight sampled profiles passed the added hold check.

The CPU profiles exercise the accumulator register; they do not capture IO. Two standalone memory profiles cover both IO registers. The smallest observed hold interval is 252.5 ns, versus the alternative part's 3 ns requirement. Rows with zero events provide no hold coverage.

This bounded study does not qualify minimum silicon delays, physical loads, all corner profiles or full boot. Any substitution needs revised schematic/BOM identity and accepted timing evidence, including a physical load limit of 50 pF for these bounds.
